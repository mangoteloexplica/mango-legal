Mango Videos - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Who we are
Mango Videos is a personal tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account "Mango te lo explica". Contact: mangoteloexplica@gmail.com

2. What data the app accesses
When the owner connects their own TikTok account, the app receives an access token and basic profile identifiers (such as the account's open ID). The app uses them only to send the owner's own videos to TikTok.

3. How data is stored
The access token is stored locally on the owner's computer. It is not uploaded to any server and is not shared with anyone.

4. What we do not do
The app does not collect personal data from other people, does not track users, does not sell or share data, and does not use data for advertising.

5. Retention and deletion
The token can be deleted at any time by deleting the local file or by revoking the app's access in TikTok settings (Settings and privacy > Security & permissions > Apps and services).

6. Changes
This policy may be updated. The latest version is always available at this page.
